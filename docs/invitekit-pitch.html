InviteKit

Privacy-first invitations for people who host a lot.

Partiful and Evite are free because you're the product — your guest list gets harvested and advertised against. InviteKit is the opposite: clean, no ads, no guest accounts, and open-source, so we can't sell your data.

The wedge isn't one-off party-throwers — they'll use whatever's free and viral. It's recurring hosts: clubs, classes, congregations, communities running many events for the same group. They want co-organizers, a member list that persists, and their own branding — a real need the viral apps ignore, and a group that will pay.

Model. Hosted SaaS is the business; open-source is the moat — trust and a marketing funnel, not a market we serve.

Status. Live and working (free-vite.com) — events, RSVPs, magic-link guests, bulk email. Single-tenant today; multi-tenant white-label is next.
