InviteKit FORMERLY SOIRÉE

Privacy-first invitations for people who host a lot.

You know this app as Soirée — the one we ran the band's show on last March. InviteKit is that same app, generalized. Same magic-link RSVPs and clean guest experience, now built to run any host's events under any brand. Soirée was the first night out; this is the product it grew into.

Partiful and Evite are free because you're the product — your guest list gets harvested and advertised against. InviteKit is the opposite: clean, no ads, no guest accounts, and open-source, so we can't sell your data.

The opportunity isn't one-off party-throwers — they'll use whatever's free and viral. It's recurring hosts: bands, clubs, classes, congregations, communities running many events for the same people. They want co-organizers, a guest list that persists across events, and their own branding — a real need the viral apps ignore, and a group that will pay.

What it does today
  • One-tap RSVPs — guests reply by magic link; no account, no app to download.
  • Guest management — add by hand or import a CSV, with smart paste of name + email.
  • Email built in — invites, reminders, nudges, thank-yous, and custom sends.
  • Live headcount — RSVPs and guest counts update in real time.
  • Comments — public notes or private messages between guest and host.
  • Your brand — name and look are configurable; private by design — the invite link is the only key.

The model. The hosted service is the business; the open-source code is the moat — it's what makes “we can't sell your data” believable, and it doubles as marketing. Live today at free-vite.com; single-tenant now, multi-tenant white-label next.
